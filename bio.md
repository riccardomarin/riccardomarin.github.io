I am an Interim Professor at the Chair of Machine Learning for Robotics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), and a member of the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML) and of ELLIS. My research aims to give machines a sense of the three-dimensional world that aligns with human perception: I work on shape correspondence, spectral geometry and geometric deep learning, with a focus on virtual humans — how they move, dress, and interact with objects.

Before, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Computer Vision Group at TUM with Daniel Cremers, at the University of Tübingen with Gerard Pons-Moll, supported by Humboldt and Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowships, and at Sapienza University of Rome with Emanuele Rodolà. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Verona under Umberto Castellani; my thesis received the EG-Italy Best PhD Thesis Award.

My work received the Best Student Paper Award at 3DV 2020, a Top Cited Paper recognition in Computer Graphics Forum, a Best Paper Award at the NeurReps workshop, and an Honorable Mention at GCPR 2026. I review for the main vision, graphics and learning venues, and received seven Outstanding Reviewer Awards.
